The case study is an illustrative analysis informed by published clinical literature. Its numerical results are synthetic. They do not represent a validated GenomeLens v1.11 model output, and they are not clinical or economic evidence.

Both documents are research communication. Neither is an independent validation of GenomeLens. The current development version, v1.11, is private; the benchmark evidence referred to in the overview describes the frozen v1 build. The overview also cites one earlier-build external comparison (the Genome in a Bottle trio); that result is labelled historical and is not representative of v1.11 validation. The two PDFs are published exactly as prepared, so their signed Content Credentials stay valid; the overview therefore carries no links into this repository. Its companion material is here: the [illustrative case study](GenomeLens_TSC_Illustrative_HEOR_Case_Study.pdf), the economic methodology in [`ECONOMICS.md`](../docs/ECONOMICS.md), the validation design and results in [`VALIDATION.md`](../docs/VALIDATION.md), the limitations in [`LIMITATIONS.md`](../docs/LIMITATIONS.md), the public test suite in [`TESTING.md`](../docs/TESTING.md) and the [Deterministic Benchmark v1](../benchmark/v1/) evidence package.
