# Architecture

A conceptual description of how a genomic observation becomes — or fails to become — a decision-relevant economic result. Stage semantics are public; production routing rules, qualification logic and parameter registries are not.

```
GENOMIC EVENT
   ↓  observable in the available modality?
   ↓  callable at this position, in this assay?
   ↓  supported by evidence of sufficient grade?
   ↓  clinically actionable?
   ↓  individually applicable to this person, now?
   ↓  economically evidenced?
   ↓
MODELLED · CONDITIONAL · REFUSED
   ↓
REPORT + PROVENANCE
```

**Observable in the available modality.** A short-read variant callset cannot speak to every class of genomic event. Copy number, structural variants, repeat expansions and some star-allele architectures are not resolvable from a SNV/indel callset alone. The pipeline records which question the input can answer rather than answering all of them at uniform confidence.

**Callable.** A position that produced no row is not automatically reference. Distinguishing *explicitly called*, *callable and reference*, *explicit no-call*, and *never interrogated* is load-bearing: the last two are not evidence of absence, and collapsing them into "negative" is how false reassurance is manufactured.

**Supported.** Evidence grade is carried with the finding, not asserted by the reporting layer.

**Clinically actionable.** Guideline actionability is a property of the variant-drug or variant-condition pair, not of the person.

**Individually applicable.** This is where actionability and applicability separate. A pharmacogenomic result is only economically live when the relevant medication or indication is actually in play; absent that, the finding is reported and the economics are withheld.

**Economically evidenced.** Each input to a monetary result carries a provenance tier: published, derived by a stated step, or declared assumption. The composition of those tiers is reported alongside the result.

**Terminal disposition.** Every tracked pathway ends in a defined state — modeled, conditional, unresolved, refused, or not economically applicable. Nothing falls through silently, and a value that could not be computed is never rendered as `$0`.

**Report and provenance.** Outputs carry the inputs, versions and scope that produced them.

Public artifacts are generated by a constrained export layer rather than by exporting a production report and removing fields — see [`PUBLIC-REPORT-SPEC.md`](PUBLIC-REPORT-SPEC.md).
